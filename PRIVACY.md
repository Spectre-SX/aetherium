# Aetherium Privacy Policy

**Last updated: [DD/MM/YYYY] 04/09/2026**

Aetherium is an open-source, self-hosted/local application.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Aetherium collects, what information is stored locally, and what information may be sent to third-party services when you choose to use them.

## 1. Information Aetherium Collects

The Aetherium project does not operate a centralized backend for the application.

Aetherium does **not** intentionally collect or receive:

- Conversations or chat history
- Prompts or messages
- API keys
- Passwords used for local profiles
- Files processed by the application
- Local application settings
- Installed plugins
- AI model configurations
- Usage analytics
- Telemetry
- Crash reports
- Download statistics
- IP addresses through an Aetherium-operated analytics system

Aetherium does not use telemetry or analytics to track how the application is used.

## 2. Local Profiles

Aetherium may allow you to create a local profile.

Information entered when creating a local profile is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to the Aetherium project.

For example, if you enter an email address while creating a local profile, that email address remains on your device.

Aetherium does not currently use email verification for local profiles.

A local profile should not be interpreted as an online account operated by the Aetherium project.

If you delete your local Aetherium data, locally stored profile information will be deleted according to how your operating system or Aetherium installation handles that data.

## 3. Conversations and Application Data

Aetherium is designed to keep application data on the user's device or self-hosted environment.

Depending on your configuration, this may include:

- Conversations
- Prompts
- Responses
- Application settings
- API keys
- Provider configurations
- Plugin configurations
- Model configurations
- Local profile information
- Other application data

This information is not intentionally transmitted to Aetherium's project maintainers.

You are responsible for securing your own device, filesystem, backups, and self-hosted environment.

## 4. Third-Party AI Providers

Aetherium may allow you to connect to third-party AI providers.

Examples may include:

- OpenAI
- Anthropic
- Google
- Other providers supported by Aetherium

When you use an AI provider, information required to perform the request may be sent to that provider.

Depending on the provider and your configuration, this may include:

- Prompts
- Messages
- Conversation context
- Files or attachments
- Model configuration
- Other information included in your request

These requests are made as part of your use of the third-party provider and are not Aetherium collecting that information for its own purposes.

Third-party providers have their own privacy policies, terms, and data-handling practices.

You should review the privacy policy and terms of any provider you choose to use.

Aetherium does not control how third-party providers process information.

## 5. API Keys

Aetherium may allow you to enter API keys for third-party services.

API keys are intended to be stored locally as part of your Aetherium configuration.

Aetherium does not intentionally transmit your API keys to the Aetherium project.

However, your API keys may be used to authenticate requests to the third-party service for which they were created.

You are responsible for keeping your API keys secure and for understanding the security implications of storing them on your device.

## 6. Plugins and Extensions

Aetherium may allow you to install third-party plugins or extensions.

Plugins are created and maintained by their respective developers and may behave differently from the Aetherium core application.

Depending on their functionality, plugins may:

- Access locally available information
- Store information on your device
- Communicate with external services
- Send information to their developers or other third parties
- Process files or other local data

The Aetherium project does not guarantee the privacy practices of third-party plugins.

You should review the source code, documentation, privacy policy, and other information provided by a plugin developer before installing a plugin.

## 7. Marketplace

The Aetherium Marketplace is primarily a catalog of links to third-party repositories and resources.

The catalog may contain links to GitHub repositories or other third-party locations.

The Aetherium project does not operate the third-party repositories linked through the marketplace.

When you visit or download content from a third-party repository, that third party may process technical information associated with your request according to its own policies.

For example, if a marketplace entry links to GitHub, your interaction with GitHub is subject to GitHub's own privacy practices.

Aetherium does not control the privacy practices of third-party websites, repositories, plugins, models, or services.

## 8. AI Models

Aetherium may provide a catalog containing links to third-party AI models.

AI models may have their own licenses, terms, or requirements.

Downloading or using a model may involve interacting with a third-party website, repository, or hosting provider.

Any information processed by those third parties is governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

## 9. GitHub

The Aetherium project may be hosted on GitHub.

GitHub may independently collect and process information when you visit repositories, download files, submit issues, create pull requests, or otherwise interact with GitHub.

That processing is performed by GitHub and is subject to GitHub's own privacy practices.

Aetherium does not control or operate GitHub's infrastructure.

## 10. Cookies and Tracking

The Aetherium application does not intentionally use advertising trackers or analytics trackers.

Aetherium does not intentionally use cookies or similar technologies to track users across websites.

Third-party websites and services accessed through Aetherium may use cookies, analytics, logging, or other technologies according to their own policies.

## 11. Data Security

Because Aetherium is designed to operate locally or be self-hosted, users are responsible for the security of their own installations.

You are responsible for:

- Securing your device
- Protecting your local files
- Protecting API keys
- Securing backups
- Keeping software updated
- Reviewing installed plugins
- Securing any self-hosted environment

No software can guarantee absolute security.

## 12. Children's Privacy

Aetherium is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children.

Because Aetherium does not operate a centralized user-data system, it does not intentionally collect children's conversations, usage data, or analytics.

Users should comply with applicable laws and the terms of any third-party services they use with Aetherium.

## 13. Data Retention

The Aetherium project does not maintain a centralized database containing users' application data.

Data stored locally by Aetherium remains under the control of the user or operator of the installation.

The retention of locally stored data is determined by the user's device, configuration, backups, and actions.

Third-party services may retain information according to their own policies.

## 14. Data Deletion

Because Aetherium does not operate a centralized application-data service, there is generally no Aetherium server account containing your conversations, API keys, or application data that you need to request deletion from.

You can manage or delete locally stored Aetherium data through the application, operating system, or self-hosted environment, as applicable.

Information held by third-party services must be managed according to those services' respective policies and procedures.

## 15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Aetherium changes.

If the project's data practices change significantly, this document will be updated to reflect those changes.

The "Last updated" date at the beginning of this document indicates when this policy was most recently revised.

## 16. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or Aetherium's privacy practices may be submitted through:

https://github.com/Spectre-SX/aetherium/

**Summary**

Aetherium is designed to operate locally or be self-hosted.

The Aetherium project does not intentionally collect or store users' conversations, API keys, application data, telemetry, analytics, or usage information.

Information may be sent to third-party services when users explicitly configure and use those services, such as AI providers, plugins, models, GitHub repositories, or other external services.

Those third-party services are responsible for their own data practices and privacy policies.
